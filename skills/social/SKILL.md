# Social Media Content Strategy & Creation

This skill guides you through creating, optimizing, and planning social media content across platforms. It ensures consistent brand voice, audience engagement, and platform-specific best practices.

## When to Use This Skill

- **Creating posts**: Instagram captions, X threads, LinkedIn articles, TikTok scripts
- **Planning**: Content calendar, seasonal campaigns, posting schedules
- **Optimization**: Hashtag research, caption copywriting, call-to-action testing
- **Analytics**: Engagement trends, audience insights, performance analysis
- **Cross-platform**: Adapting content for different platforms with platform-specific rules

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram
- **Optimal post length**: 50-200 characters (captions can be longer, but hook in first 125 characters)
- **Hashtag strategy**: 15-25 hashtags, mix of niche + trending
- **Posting time**: 9:00-11:00 AM JST weekdays, 6:00-8:00 PM JST weekends
- **Content mix**: 60% educational, 30% inspirational, 10% call-to-action
- **Visuals**: 1080×1350px vertical, high contrast readable on mobile
- **Reels**: 15-60 seconds, hook in first 3 seconds, captions for accessibility

### X (Twitter)
- **Thread structure**: Main tweet + supporting tweets + CTA
- **Character efficiency**: Use threads for nuance; keep individual tweets under 280 chars
- **Hashtags**: 1-3 per tweet
- **Engagement**: Reply to quote tweets within 24 hours

### LinkedIn
- **Tone**: Professional, thought leadership
- **Length**: 100-3000 characters
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 industry-specific
- **Visual**: Carousel posts + infographics outperform plain text

## Content Creation Workflow

**Step 1: Define Objective** — Goal, target audience, desired action  
**Step 2: Draft Hook** — First line determines scroll-stop rate  
**Step 3: Build Body** — Storytelling: Situation → Challenge → Resolution  
**Step 4: Call-to-Action** — Specific, low-friction  
**Step 5: Hashtag & Metadata** — Mix high-volume + niche hashtags  
**Step 6: Schedule & Monitor** — Post, respond within 2 hours, track metrics

## Measurement & Iteration

Track weekly:
- **Reach**: Total impressions
- **Engagement rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Reach × 100%
- **Click-through rate**: Clicks / Impressions × 100%
- **Conversion**: Target action completion

If engagement drops below 2%, adjust: timing, hook language, hashtags, CTA clarity.
