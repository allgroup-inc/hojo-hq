# Humanizer: Natural Language & Brand Voice Polish

This skill guides you through transforming formal, stiff, or AI-sounding text into natural, engaging, brand-aligned language. It covers tone adjustment, jargon removal, personality injection, and cultural fit.

## When to Use This Skill

- **AI-generated text polish**: Remove robotic phrasing ("It is imperative that...", "Please be advised...")
- **Formal→casual conversion**: Email, social post, or marketing copy that feels too stiff
- **Brand voice alignment**: Make text sound like "your" organization, not generic
- **Accessibility**: Simplify complex sentences, remove jargon, explain technical terms
- **Cultural adaptation**: Ensure Japanese context, idioms, references feel natural (not English-sounding Japanese)
- **Tone consistency**: Multiple writers, one voice
- **Register matching**: Adjust for audience (internal email vs. customer-facing vs. social media)

## Brand Voice for hojo-hq Projects

### 沖縄企業のミカタ (GLOW)

**Brand personality**: Supportive professional, not corporate robot  
**Audience**: Busy SME owners, non-financial-expert  
**Tone**: Knowledgeable but humble, conversational, encouraging

**✓ Use these patterns**:
- "〇〇社長へ" (personal, direct address)
- "～ことがあります" (gentle, relatable)
- "意外かもしれませんが、..." (curious, humble)
- "ぜひ活用してください" (warm encouragement)
- "わからないことがあれば、..."(supportive)

**✗ Avoid**:
- "貴社においては..." (too formal)
- "以下の通りである。" (stiff, written)
- "～と考えられる" (distant, academic)
- "お取り扱いさせていただく" (over-polite, corporate)
- Business jargon: "アジェンダ", "アクションアイテム", "スコープ"

### もらいわすれ堂 (フクギイロ)

**Brand personality**: Warm, non-judgmental companion  
**Audience**: Low-income households, elderly, information-anxious  
**Tone**: Encouraging, simple, never condescending

**✓ Use these patterns**:
- "あなたが..." (direct, personal)
- "大丈夫です。" (reassuring)
- "～できます" (empowering, not passive)
- "一緒に進めましょう" (partnership tone)
- Emoji: 🤝💚✨ (warm, not corporate)

**✗ Avoid**:
- "受給要件を満たす場合..." (bureaucratic)
- "申請プロセスは複雑である" (discouraging)
- Kanji-heavy sentences (prefer hiragana for clarity)
- "貴殿", "申し上げます", "いただく" (formal, distant)

## Rewriting Patterns

### Pattern 1: Remove Corporate Jargon

**Before**: "We will leverage synergies across our platform ecosystem to optimize stakeholder value."  
**After**: "Working together with partners, we help more business owners get the funding they deserve."

**Before**: "This initiative enables enhanced digital transformation capabilities."  
**After**: "With this system, you can apply for subsidies online in 10 minutes instead of 3 hours."

**Before**: "貴社におけるデジタルツールの導入は、業務効率化に資するものと考えられます。"  
**After**: "オンライン申請で、書類作成の時間が半分になります。"

### Pattern 2: Formal → Conversational

**Before**: "It is strongly recommended that applicants submit documentation no later than the specified deadline."  
**After**: "Deadline is next Friday. Submit early and you won't stress on the last day!"

**Before**: "ご不明な点がございましたら、お気軽にお問い合わせください。"  
**After**: "わからないことがあれば、いつでも聞いてくださいね。"

**Before**: "The aforementioned subsidy program provides financial assistance to qualifying SMEs."  
**After**: "This subsidy gives up to ¥500K to small retailers expanding their business."

### Pattern 3: Passive → Active

**Before**: "Assistance can be provided to applicants who meet the eligibility criteria."  
**After**: "We help you apply if you qualify."

**Before**: "誤った情報が提供された場合は、返納が求められることになります。"  
**After**: "間違った情報を報告すると、もらったお金を返さないといけません。"

**Before**: "It is believed that this approach will yield better outcomes."  
**After**: "This method actually works better."

### Pattern 4: Simplify Complex Sentences

**Before**: "The interdependencies between policy implementation timelines and fiscal-year appropriations, while nominally distinct, create cascading impacts on benefit eligibility windows."  
**After**: "Policy changes sometimes affect when you can apply. We keep track of these changes for you."

**Before**: "本制度の申請期間は、予算成立後3ヶ月以内であり、且つ当会計年度末までの間となる。"  
**After**: "申請期間は、予算が決まってから3ヶ月間。年度末までです。"

### Pattern 5: Explain Technical Terms

**Before**: "Applicants must satisfy the EBITDA threshold and demonstrate positive cash flow in Q3-Q4."  
**After**: "You need to show: (1) Your business made money in Q3-Q4, and (2) You have enough cash left after expenses."

**Before**: "セルフ・スターティングの中小企業者" (unclear)  
**After**: "自分で事業を始めた中小企業の経営者" (clear)

### Pattern 6: Inject Personality

**Before**: "Our application process is streamlined."  
**After**: "We made the application super simple—most people finish in 10 minutes."

**Before**: "We are committed to supporting small business growth."  
**After**: "Small businesses are the heartbeat of Okinawa. We're here to help you thrive."

**Before**: "公開サイトにて情報を掲載しております。"  
**After**: "毎日新しい制度情報を公開中です。ぜひチェックしてください！"

### Pattern 7: Cultural Fit (English→Japanese)

**Before**: "Let's sync up next week on this." (too English-idiomatic)  
**After**: "来週改めて相談させてもらえますか。"

**Before**: "I think we need to circle back to that point." (confusing in Japanese)  
**After**: "その点もう一度確認しましょう。"

**Before**: "Thanks for your patience on this." (literally translated feels awkward)  
**After**: "このまま対応いただいてありがとうございます。"

## Tone Checklist

Before finalizing any text, run through these checks:

### For Customer-Facing (hojo-hq site, social, email)
- [ ] Is the first sentence engaging? (Not: "Our mission is to...", Yes: "Did you know...")
- [ ] Would a busy SME owner understand it? (Test with simple word count < 12 avg)
- [ ] Is there at least one benefit/why? (Not just features)
- [ ] Is call-to-action clear & easy? ("Click here" not "Proceed to portal")
- [ ] Does it match our colors/tone guides? (Check docs/ブランドガイド.md)
- [ ] Would I read this on my phone? (Scan-test: break into short paragraphs)

### For Internal (Slack, Google Docs, emails to team)
- [ ] Am I being respectful of time? (Short > long, structured > wall-of-text)
- [ ] Is tone appropriate to context? (Quick update ≠ deep analysis)
- [ ] Did I avoid all-caps, double-punctuation, excessive emoji?
- [ ] Would anyone reading this 3 months later understand it? (context-agnostic check)

### For Note/Blog Posts
- [ ] Does headline hook the reader? (Question, stat, surprising fact, benefit)
- [ ] Is first 100 words compelling? (If reader stops, they got the idea)
- [ ] Did I use short paragraphs? (3-5 sentences max, then whitespace)
- [ ] Did I break up text with: headers, bullets, bold? (Scan-friendly)
- [ ] Did I end with clear CTA or next step? (Not: "If you have questions..." Yes: "LINE登録で通知受け取る")

## Common Mistakes

1. **Over-formality**: "申し上げます" when "言います" is better
2. **Mixed register**: Formal opening, then suddenly casual (inconsistent)
3. **Unfamiliar idioms**: English phrases that don't translate naturally to Japanese
4. **Wall of text**: Multiple paragraphs of 10+ sentences (unreadable on mobile)
5. **Assumed knowledge**: "EBITDA threshold" without explaining
6. **Vague CTA**: "Let us know your thoughts" vs. "Reply with your situation"
7. **Emoji overload**: More than 2-3 per social post (looks unprofessional)
8. **Negative framing**: "Don't miss" vs. "Grab" (prefer positive)
9. **Unclear pronouns**: "It has several benefits..." (what is "it"?)
10. **Too casual for formal context**: "Ya" or "gonna" in official communication
