# Cold Email & Outreach Sequences

This skill guides you through writing high-response cold emails, structuring multi-touch sequences, and personalizing outreach at scale while respecting recipient preferences and legal compliance.

## When to Use This Skill

- **Initial outreach**: First email to a prospect or contact
- **Sales sequences**: Multi-email follow-up campaigns
- **Subject line optimization**: A/B testing, open rate improvement
- **Personalization**: Custom angles for different prospect segments
- **Sequence timing**: Cadence, day/time optimization, breakoff points
- **Objection handling**: Email responses to "not interested", "budget", "timing"

## Email Structure: 3-Email Sequence

### Email 1: Awareness Hook (Day 0)
- **Subject line**: 5-8 words, curiosity or specificity (no generic "partnership opportunity")
- **Body length**: 50-75 words (mobile-scannable)
- **Structure**:
  1. **Personalized opening**: Specific reference to their work/network/recent news (never generic "I noticed you...")
  2. **Problem statement**: Single problem they likely face (subsidy deadline misses, compliance complexity)
  3. **Single CTA**: "5-min call?" or "Quick question?" — low-friction
- **Close**: First name + title (no "Best regards, Claude")

### Email 2: Value Proof (Day 3-5)
- **Subject line**: "RE: 沖縄補助金情報" or question ("Still worth 5 mins?")
- **Body length**: 75-100 words
- **Structure**:
  1. **Reference previous email**: "Following up on..."
  2. **Proof/data**: 1-2 metrics (e.g., "252 subsidies + 23 deadline alerts sent this month")
  3. **Social proof**: 1 success story (anonymized) or client quote
  4. **Same CTA**: Call, coffee, quick chat
- **PS**: Optional second CTA or specific hook ("沖縄版Q4補助金速報を先行送信できます")

### Email 3: Breakoff (Day 7-10)
- **Subject line**: Final angle ("Final message") or new insight
- **Body length**: 50 words (shorter = higher open rate)
- **Structure**:
  1. **Acknowledge radio silence**: "No pressure if the timing's off..."
  2. **New angle or escalation**: Different value prop or lower-barrier CTA (vs. "call" → "share your biggest subsidy challenge")
  3. **Soft breakoff**: "Will respect your silence and check back in Q4" or "Open if anything changes"
- **No aggressive tone** — respect is more profitable long-term

## Sequence Timing & Frequency

| Sequence | Timing | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Email 1 | Tuesday 9:00-10:00 AM JST | High open rates, morning check |
| Email 2 | Thursday 2:00-3:00 PM JST | Enough gap, high afternoon engagement |
| Email 3 | Next Tuesday 10:00 AM JST | 7-10 days creates urgency without spam feel |

**Do NOT send more than 3 unprompted emails.** After 3, wait for response signal (open, click, reply).

## Subject Line Rules

**High open rate rules**:
- ✓ Specific number or data ("252件", "3ヶ月で")
- ✓ Recipient name or company ("〇〇さん向け")
- ✓ Curiosity question ("知ってましたか？")
- ✓ Urgency without all-caps ("Q4申請の締切")
- ✗ Generic ("Partnership opportunity", "Quick question")
- ✗ Spammy words ("FREE!", "URGENT", "Act now!!!")
- ✗ Too long (>50 characters on mobile)

**A/B testing**: Test 2 subject lines across 100 prospects each. Pick winner for remaining 1,000.

## Legal & Compliance

1. **Unsubscribe**: Every email must have clear "unsubscribe" or "do not contact" footer
2. **Identification**: Sign with actual name + title + company, not "Claude" or bot aliases
3. **Data source**: Only email addresses from public directories, LinkedIn, referrals
4. **Do not scrape**: Avoid mass email tools that hide your identity
5. **CAN-SPAM (US) / GDPR (EU)**: hojo-hq operates in Japan (APPI), but respect opt-outs globally
6. **One email per person**: Do not email same person under different addresses or names

## Handling Responses

| Response Type | Action | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| "Not interested" | Mark as unsubscribe, do NOT re-email | None (respect no-contact) |
| "Call me Thursday" | Schedule call, prepare agenda | Confirm + agenda 1 hour before |
| "Can you send more info?" | Brief PDF/1-pager (not 50 pages) + call offer | Info + soft CTA for call |
| No response after 3 | End sequence, mark as "follow up Q4" | None (respect silence) |
| "Who's Claude?" | Clarify you're an AI helping outreach, human signs off | Human closes from here |

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate**: Goal 25%+ (industry avg 15-20%)
- **Click rate**: Goal 3%+ (industry avg 1-2%)
- **Reply rate**: Goal 5%+ (industry avg 1-2%)
- **Call booked**: Goal 1%+ (industry avg 0.2-0.5%)
- **Conversion (referral/sale)**: Goal 0.5%+

If open rate < 15%, change subject line. If reply rate < 1%, change value prop or sequence length.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Wall of text**: Emails > 200 words have <10% reply rate
2. **Too many links**: 1-2 links max; "click here for more" = ignored
3. **Asking for too much**: "30-min call" first email = no responses. Start with "5 mins"
4. **Generic opening**: "I noticed your company does X" = spam signal
5. **Missing unsubscribe**: Legal risk + looks unprofessional
6. **Sending from no-reply**: Responses go nowhere
7. **No social proof**: Data > opinions; "252 subsidies processed" > "we're great"
8. **Wrong contact**: Send to CEO when decision-maker is CFO = wasted email
