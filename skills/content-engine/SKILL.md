# Content Engine: Long-Form Strategy & Creation

This skill guides you through planning, outlining, and writing long-form content that ranks in search, engages readers, and drives conversions. It covers blogs, whitepapers, guides, SEO articles, and serialized content series.

## When to Use This Skill

- **Blog posts & articles**: SEO-optimized, 1,500-5,000 words
- **Whitepapers & guides**: In-depth, educational, 3,000-10,000 words
- **Ebooks & mini-courses**: Serialized, multi-part content
- **Newsletters**: Regular updates, curated content, thought leadership
- **Content series**: Related articles on a theme (e.g., "補助金Q&A" 全12話)
- **Repurposing**: One piece of content → blog + infographic + short video + social posts

## Content Strategy Framework

### Phase 1: Topic Research & Planning

**1. Keyword research**
- Use: Ubersuggest, SEMrush, Google Trends, YouTube search suggestions
- For hojo-hq: "沖縄 助成金 〇〇" + long-tail keywords ("沖縄 小売業 補助金")
- Search volume goal: 100-1,000 monthly searches (competitive but winnable)
- Keyword density: 1-2% in final draft (natural, not forced)

**2. Competitor analysis**
- Who ranks #1-3 for this keyword?
- What's their angle? (News, how-to, comparison, opinion)
- How long is their content? (Word count)
- What's missing? (Your unique angle)

**3. Create outline**
- Main sections: 3-5 top-level headings (h2)
- Sub-sections: 2-3 supporting headings per section (h3)
- Logical flow: Problem → Context → Solution → Action
- Hook in first 100 words: "読むべき理由" を明示

### Phase 2: Outline Template

```
# ブログタイトル (Target keyword in H1)

## 導入 (Introduction)
- Hook: Why should reader care? (1-2 sentences)
- Promise: What will they learn? (1 sentence)
- Length: 50-100 words

## Section 1: Context / Problem
- Background info reader needs
- Why this matters to them
- Statistics or data point

## Section 2: Solution / How-To
- Step-by-step explanation
- Examples specific to Okinawa (if applicable)
- Common mistakes to avoid

## Section 3: Implementation / Next Steps
- Concrete action items
- Resources (links, checklists, templates)
- CTA: "沖縄企業のミカタで診断してみる" (for hojo-hq)

## FAQ (optional)
- 3-5 most common questions
- 1-2 sentence answers

## 終わりに (Conclusion)
- Recap key takeaway
- Inspire action
- CTA (gentle, not pushy)
```

### Phase 3: Copywriting

**Hook (first 100 words)**
- Question: "締切まであと3週間、この補助金知ってますか？"
- Stat: "沖縄企業の73%が受け取り忘れている給付金とは"
- Story: "あるカフェ経営者が月20万円受け取った話"
- Benefit: "この記事で得られること: 〇〇、△△、□□"

**Body paragraphs**
- One idea per paragraph
- Topic sentence first (summarize in 1 line)
- 2-4 supporting sentences (explain, example, data)
- Whitespace: Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences max)
- Formatting: Bold key terms, bullet lists for multiple items

**Conclusion CTA**
- Soft: "この制度で質問がある方は..."
- Medium: "沖縄企業のミカタで詳しく確認できます"
- Strong: "LINE登録して締切アラート受け取る" (conversion goal)

### Phase 4: SEO Optimization

**On-page SEO checklist**:
- [ ] Target keyword in H1 (title)
- [ ] Target keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Target keyword in at least one H2
- [ ] Meta description (160 chars) includes target keyword
- [ ] Internal links: 2-4 links to related hojo-hq content
- [ ] External links: 1-2 links to authority sources (政府サイト, 沖縄県庁)
- [ ] Alt text on images: "沖縄 補助金 申請フロー" (not "image123.png")
- [ ] URL slug: lowercase, hyphens, target keyword ("okinawa-subsidy-checklist")
- [ ] Word count: 1,500-3,000 words for competitive keywords

**Off-page SEO**:
- Promote on social: Instagram + note + Twitter
- Earn backlinks: Pitch to 士業ブログ, 商工会 resources
- Internal linking: Cross-link from related posts
- Email to subscribers: "New guide: 沖縄の〇〇補助金"

### Phase 5: Multi-Format Adaptation

One long-form piece → 5 repurposed formats:

| Format | Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Blog (3,000 words) | hojo-hq site + note | Full article |
| Infographic | Instagram + Pinterest | Top 5 key points visual |
| Checklist (PDF) | Email + opt-in | 1-page printable version |
| Social thread (X) | Twitter | 5-tweet version with key insights |
| Short video script | TikTok/Shorts | 60-sec summary + CTA |

## Measurement & Iteration

**Track per piece**:
- **Page views**: Google Analytics (goal: 100+ in first month)
- **Time on page**: Goal > 2 minutes (engaged reading)
- **Click-through rate**: To LINE, referral, or next article (goal: 3%+)
- **Conversion**: Did readers sign up, contact 士業, take next action?

**Track per series (month)**:
- **Total traffic**: 500+ page views/month (growing)
- **Return visitors**: 30%+ returning traffic
- **Organic search**: 50%+ traffic from Google (not paid)
- **Email subscribers**: Growth rate, open rate, unsubscribe rate

If page views < 50 in first month:
- Check SEO: Is keyword ranking? (Google Search Console)
- Check traffic source: Did you promote it?
- Check content: Is it useful vs. competitors?

## Common Mistakes

1. **No hook**: Reader doesn't know why they should keep reading
2. **Too long intro**: Get to the point by paragraph 2
3. **Keyword stuffing**: "沖縄助成金沖縄補助金沖縄..." = unnatural, Google penalizes
4. **No internal links**: Miss opportunity to keep readers on site
5. **Outdated data**: 2 years old statistics = not credible (always date your sources)
6. **Complex sentences**: Average sentence length > 20 words = harder to read
7. **No CTA**: Reader finishes article, doesn't know next step
8. **Not mobile-optimized**: 60% read on phone; paragraph width too wide = frustration
9. **No proofreading**: Typos = lose credibility on a finance/benefits topic
10. **Forgetting formatting**: Wall of text = 80% skip after headline. Use headers, bullets, bold.
